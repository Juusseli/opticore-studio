# Code of conduct

OptiCore Studio is an engineering project. We want discussions about optics,
physics and software to be precise, open and friendly to everyone, whatever their
background or level of experience.

## Expected

* Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in
  a second language.
* Argue with evidence: references, measurements, test cases.
* Give and accept technical criticism graciously. The goal is correct results, not
  winning an argument.
* Credit the work and the sources of others.

## Not acceptable

* Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
* Discriminatory language or imagery.
* Publishing other people's private information.
* Posting proprietary code, data or documents that you have no right to share.
* Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and every other
project space. Report a problem privately to the maintainer; the contact details
are on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or ban a participant temporarily or
permanently, for conduct that breaks this code.
