# Sample photos for the live image

Both files were scaled to 640 px on the longer side and re-encoded as JPEG
(quality 88, metadata removed). They are used as built-in scenes of the *Live
image* view and by the start-screen examples (backend `samples.py`:
`live-eiffel`, `live-pillars`).

| File | Subject | Author | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `eiffel-tower.jpg` | Looking up the centre of the Eiffel Tower (23 October 2011) | Jebulon | CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication) | [Wikimedia Commons: Sous la Tour Eiffel 1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sous_la_Tour_Eiffel_1.jpg) |
| `pillars-of-creation.jpg` | The Pillars of Creation in the Eagle Nebula (Hubble, 1995) | NASA, Jeff Hester and Paul Scowen (Arizona State University) | Public domain (NASA material; credit NASA as the source) | [Wikimedia Commons: Eagle nebula pillars.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eagle_nebula_pillars.jpg) |

The Eiffel Tower itself (Gustave Eiffel, died 1923) is in the public domain.
